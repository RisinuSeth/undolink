LINKS WITH A LIFESPAN

A temporary link generator for sharing on your terms.

Sometimes a link only needs to work for a little while. UndoLink creates a temporary redirect URL that you can share, with controls for its timing and access. The original destination stays as it is; UndoLink controls how long its redirect remains available.

Temporary link questions

What is a temporary link?

A temporary link is a URL designed to remain usable only for a defined period or until a selected usage limit is reached. UndoLink creates temporary redirect links with optional expiry, click and access controls.

Can I make a URL expire at a specific time?

Yes. Choose a preset duration or set a custom date and time for the redirect to stop working.

Can I password-protect an expiring link?

Yes. Add an optional password when creating the link. Visitors need the password before they can continue to the destination.

Does UndoLink delete the original destination?

No. UndoLink controls its own redirect. When the temporary link expires or reaches its click limit, the original destination is not deleted or changed.
